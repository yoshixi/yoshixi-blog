# Reflection on 2019

My first year as a new graduate taught me that work is fundamentally about people moving together.  
Execution matters, but consistently moving and caring for each other mattered even more.

In a small company without fully established systems, ownership boundaries are often blurry.  
Because of that, frequent alignment was essential; otherwise we quickly drifted apart.

Another lesson: we were all young and inexperienced, and there was no superhero to rescue us.  
We had to avoid overestimating each other and proactively communicate so we would not reach crisis mode.

I also felt my perspective shift this year.  
In the previous year, I focused mostly on visible personal skill growth.  
In 2019, I started looking more at business, organization, and systems.

## Things that worked

- Running almost every day
- Writing regular reflections in words

These habits helped me maintain both focus and mental stability.

## Challenges

- Still weak at thinking in longer horizons
- Need stronger execution quality and speed
- Need better delegation and scalable structures
- Need to build structural leverage through technology

## For 2020

I want to set milestones in multi-month spans, build stronger organizational systems, and focus on work that only I can do.
