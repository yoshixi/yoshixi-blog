Every year-end I want to write a reflection post, but I also feel my energy for writing has slowly cooled down over time.

Still, looking back at old posts each year is meaningful, so I kept typing this one too.

## Events in 2021

### Moved to Osaki

I moved in May.  
The biggest upgrade was sunlight in the new apartment — it changed how I feel in the morning more than I expected.

### Started working with two companies as a contractor

From August, I was involved with two companies.  
It gave me direct exposure to how different organizations operate at different growth phases.

I also felt that my technical ability had reached a level where teams could trust me to contribute reliably.

### Restarted running

In December I restarted running seriously after a long gap.  
Tracking pace and distance with an Apple Watch made it fun enough to continue.

## What decreased this year

- Journaling
- Reading books
- Going to museums

I spent most free time on programming.  
For next year, I want to bring back more non-capitalistic, cultural activities into my life.

## Toward next year

I want to find something I can deeply focus on again.  
Thank you for reading, and see you next year.
