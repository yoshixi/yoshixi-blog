I am writing my yearly reflection again this year.

## Work

I entered my second year at my current company.  
Because the organization is not very large, one year was enough to understand a good part of how things work.

### First trip to the US

In February, I visited the US for the first time.  
Everything felt big, rough, and expensive, but being in a place with different values and language gave me useful perspective.

I also improved my TOEIC score from around 500 in university to over 800 after joining the company.

### Software development

From April, I moved to a project for construction and railway domains.  
I had more opportunities to care deeply about software quality and receive reviews from very experienced engineers.

My mindset shifted from:

- "Ship now, improve later if needed"

to:

- "Implement required things with high quality from the start"

## Private life

I traveled to Matsumoto, Hamamatsu, and Sapporo for the first time.  
In the second half of the year, I trained running much more seriously and ran a half marathon.

I had aimed for 1,000 km in 2023, but ended up running around 2,000 km.

## Looking ahead to 2024

2023 was less about starting brand-new challenges and more about steady continuity:

- running
- English learning
- software development

I want to continue building that slow and consistent momentum next year.
