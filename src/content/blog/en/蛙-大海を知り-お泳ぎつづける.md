I am the kind of person who often wants to start something new.  
But recently, I have noticed hesitation inside myself.

- What if I cannot secure time?
- What if I cannot continue?
- What if I do not improve much?
- What if I improve, then feel crushed by stronger people?

These fears make me avoid starting at all.

I know challenges are good for me.  
Still, beginning from zero always has a high cost, and I sometimes want easier options.

As we gain knowledge and experience, we also gain a sharper sense of difficulty.  
That knowledge protects us, but it can also become a chain.

Even so, I want to remain someone who keeps challenging things — even small challenges.

Comfort is not evil.  
But I do not want to stop myself just because something is hard.

Results matter.  
And sometimes, even after getting results, seeing a bigger ocean can be discouraging.

Still, I want to jump into that ocean.  
I want to believe the process of reaching for something beyond what I know is what makes life richer.
