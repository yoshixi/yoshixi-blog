## Notion introduced a new concept in document software

Notion changed the category not by adding random features, but by introducing the concept of **Blocks**.

That single abstraction unlocked many UX capabilities and made broad feature expansion possible.

## Why "Block" mattered technically

As an engineer, implementing a block-first product raises hard problems:

- polymorphic-like data modeling in DB
- complex query patterns
- complex rendering logic in frontend depending on block type

## Why this became feasible

I think the following technical shifts made it practical:

1. Cloud and database evolution made scaling for heavier queries easier.
2. Declarative frontend frameworks (like React) made complex UI logic manageable.

## Takeaway

When development environments evolve, new product concepts become implementable — and those concepts can reshape UX.

That is how a newcomer can outperform incumbents with better UX even if feature lists look similar.

From the incumbent side, continuously updating technology is not only about developer motivation, but also about retaining users.
