# 2020 and 2021

In 2020, I worked in two different contexts at once: my company and contract work.  
It felt like having one foot in full-time employment and one foot in freelance-style work.

## What happened in 2020

- Started contract work in February
- Went fully remote in April
- Joined product operations in June
- Joined new product development in August
- Returned to operations in October

## Contract work lessons

It was my first time building software for an external product.  
I was unsure whether I could adapt to another team's development style, but collaboration went well and I felt I delivered value.

I also learned that engineering is fortunate: knowledge is often available online.  
Even if your company lacks internal know-how, you can still grow by creating good output opportunities.

## Reflections

My biggest issue this year was shallowness in several areas, especially communication quality and practical decision-making.

I was also reminded that "simple" and "rough" are different:

- Rough implementation may be acceptable for early hypothesis testing
- But if maintained too long, it becomes expensive and fragile

## Toward 2021

I want to build a healthier long-term rhythm in life and relationships, while continuing to improve technically and professionally.
