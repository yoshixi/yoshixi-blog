A line from Haruki Murakami stayed with me:

> I think while I write. I do not simply write what I have already thought. Through writing, I shape my thought. By rewriting, I deepen it.

For a long time, I thought I needed a strong idea first in order to write a blog post.  
Now I think the opposite is often true.

Writing itself creates thought.

So I want to start by typing first, and discover ideas through the process.
